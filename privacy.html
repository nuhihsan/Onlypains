OnlyPains privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026

OnlyPains is a free, personal gym log made by Nuh for himself and his friends. This page explains what the app keeps and why.

Without an account

Everything you log (workouts, runs, food, body measurements and settings) stays in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

With an account

If you sign in, your log is copied to a private database (Cloudflare D1) so you can use it on other devices and get it back after reinstalling. Only your account can read it. We store:

  • your username, and a salted hash of your password if you set one (never the password itself)
  • if you use Google sign-in: your Google account ID and email address, used only to sign you in
  • the workout, run, food, body and settings data you enter in the app

We don't use your data for advertising, don't sell it and don't share it with anyone.

Friends and groups

If you add friends or join a group, the people you're connected with can see a summary of your stats: your username, recent workouts (exercises, sets, volume, top sets and PRs), best lifts, streak, recent runs and weekly totals. Body weight is only shown if you switch it on. You can switch workouts, runs and body weight off at any time under Friends > What friends can see.

Friends never see your full log, your food, your other body measurements or your settings. People who aren't your friends and aren't in a group with you see nothing. Removing a friend or leaving a group stops sharing with them straight away.

Feedback

Messages you send from the Feedback tab go to the developer on Telegram, with the name you type, your username if signed in, and the app version.

Deleting your data

Signing out removes your data from that device. To delete your account and everything stored with it, send a request through the Feedback tab or contact @nuh420 on Telegram, and it will be deleted.
